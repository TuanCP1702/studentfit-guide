Cheap Recipes Under $5 Per Serving

Fast, filling meals made with oats, eggs, canned beans, and frozen vegetables.

Nutritious Meals on a College Budget

Here are quick budget-friendly recipes made with simple ingredients like oats, eggs, canned beans, and frozen vegetables. Each recipe costs under five dollars per serving and can be made in big batches for the week ahead. Learn how to cook tasty meals like one-pan rice bowls, microwave mug meals, and easy overnight oats.

Why Batch Cooking Saves Time During Exams

Making three or four portions at once means you only wash dishes once while securing ready-to-eat lunches between back-to-back lectures. Every recipe below requires 10 minutes or less of active prep time.

Top 3 Student Staple Recipes

~$1.20 / Serving
Peanut Butter Overnight Oats

Ingredients: 1/2 cup rolled oats, 1/2 cup milk, 1 tbsp peanut butter, 1/2 sliced banana.

Stir in a jar before bed and refrigerate overnight for a grab-and-go 8 AM class breakfast.

~$2.40 / Serving
One-Pan Fiesta Rice Bowl

Ingredients: 1 cup cooked rice, 1/2 cup black beans, frozen corn, salsa, shredded cheese.

Combine in a skillet or microwave-safe bowl for 2 minutes. High fiber and 18g protein.

~$1.85 / Serving
2-Minute Microwave Egg Mug

Ingredients: 2 eggs, splash of milk, handful of frozen spinach, pinch of salt & pepper.

Whisk inside a large microwave-safe mug and cook in 45-second bursts until fluffy.
