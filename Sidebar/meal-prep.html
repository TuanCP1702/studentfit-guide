Student Meal Prep on a Budget

Learn the 3-step formula to eat healthy, save $50+ a week, and avoid late-night fast food.

Healthy Eating Without Breaking Your Semester Budget

Eating well in college does not have to cost a fortune or take up all your free time. This section breaks down the basics of meal planning, buying groceries on a budget, and keeping healthy food in your room. Check out our sub-pages for specific budget recipes and smart prep strategies made for dorm living.

The $25 Weekly Student Grocery Formula

Instead of buying expensive single-serve snacks, focus on versatile whole-food staples that mix and match across breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Buying store-brand oats, brown rice, canned black beans, eggs, bananas, and frozen broccoli gives you 12+ balanced meals for roughly $25.

3 Steps to Weekly Meal Prep Success

Step 1
Pick 2 Base Carbs

Choose affordable energy sources like rolled oats, microwaveable rice pouches, or whole-wheat pasta.

Step 2
Add Budget Protein

Stock up on eggs, canned tuna, Greek yogurt, peanut butter, or canned chickpeas and black beans.

Step 3
Use Frozen Veggies

Frozen spinach, mixed vegetables, and berries never spoil quickly in a mini fridge and steam in minutes.
